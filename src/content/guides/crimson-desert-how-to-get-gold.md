# How to Get Gold in Crimson Desert

## Overview

Gold is one of the most important resources for upgrades, trading and progression.

## Best Early Sources

Early gold usually comes from enemy camps, loot containers and quest rewards.

## Best Farming Strategy

Focus on repeatable combat areas, high-value loot and vendor routes.

## Tips

Do not waste gold early on low-value upgrades.
