# Best Gold Farming Route in Crimson Desert

## Overview

This guide explains efficient gold farming routes for early and mid game.

## Best Early Route

Focus on enemy camps with quick loot resets and vendor-friendly drops.

## Best Mid Game Route

Use denser combat zones with stronger enemies and better loot conversion.

## Tips

Avoid long travel loops and prioritize areas with both gold and upgrade materials.
